    Can you fix the problems for me?

    We give you everything to fix them: the steps, the exact text to paste and ready-to-install files for whoever runs your website. We never ask for your logins, so you stay in control of every listing.

    I'm already #1 on Google. Do I need this?

    Showing up first on Google and being named by AI are different. AI pulls from many sites plus its own judgment, and it skips businesses that are #1 on Google every day. Your report shows where you stand.

    What if AI already names my business?

    Then your report is good news, and it's free. Most owners still find their hours or phone wrong somewhere, which is what makes AI stop naming them later.

    Which AI assistants do you check?

    The AI assistants your customers use, each through its official service with web search on, the way a customer would ask. The audit asks every question on every assistant we check. Your report lists exactly which ones we asked and when.

    How long does it take?

    Not long. Your private report link appears the moment you send the form, and it fills in as the AI answers come back. The $49 audit starts the moment you pay. Email is optional either way.

    Why might I see something different?

    AI answers change day to day and person to person. That's why we ask more than one assistant, show every answer and date each one.
