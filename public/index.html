Common questions

Is the report really free?

Yes. The scan and the report cost you nothing. If your report comes back clean, you’ll never pay us a cent. We only make money when a business has problems and wants us to fix them.

Do I have to give you my passwords or logins?

No. We only look at public information — what the AI assistants say and what your public listings show. We never need access to any of your accounts.

I’m not technical. Will I understand the report?

That’s the whole point. Every report is written in plain English: what we found, why it matters, and what to do about it. No jargon, we promise.

How long does the scan take?

Usually about a day. We’ll email you as soon as your report is ready.

What if I want you to fix things instead of doing it myself?
