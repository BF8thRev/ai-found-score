Questions

What is AI Found Score?

AI Found Score is a free report for local businesses. It shows whether the AI assistants your customers use name your business when someone in your town asks for your type of service, who it names instead, and how many problems we found.

Is it really free?

Yes. The scan and the snapshot cost nothing. You only pay if you want the $49 AI Visibility Audit, or Be the Answer ($499) to keep checking all year.

I'm already #1 on Google. Do I need this?

Showing up first on Google and being named by AI are different games. AI pulls from multiple sites plus its own judgment — businesses that are #1 on Google get skipped by AI every day. The report shows exactly where you stand.

What if AI already names my business?

Then the report is good news, and it's free. Most owners also find their hours or phone are wrong somewhere — which is exactly what makes AI stop naming them later.

Why isn't there a monthly plan on this page?

Because we don't sell protection before we've proven we can find threats. It's offered after your audit is delivered.

Do you need my logins?

No. We only read public information. We never log in to your accounts.

Will I understand it?

Yes. It's written in plain English and made to read on your phone: what AI said, who it named, and what to fix first.

How long does it take?

About one business day. Leave an email after you see your questions and we send you a private link when your report is ready.

Which AI assistants do you check?

The free snapshot asks three of the AI assistants your customers use. The AI Visibility Audit asks every assistant we check, with all 5 questions. Each one gets the same questions a customer in your town would ask, and every answer is recorded word for word. Your report lists exactly which assistants we asked and when.

Which websites does AI trust?

Every website an assistant cites in its answer goes in your AI Visibility Audit, so you can see where AI gets its information about businesses like yours. We also read your own website the way an AI crawler does, and check your Google listing’s name, phone and address against it. Yelp, Apple Maps and Bing are being added.

Can you fix the problems for me?

Yes, with Be the Answer. We submit corrections everywhere we can without your login: Google (suggest an edit), Apple Maps (report an issue), Yelp, Bing, and the major data aggregators other sites copy from. Suggesting isn’t guaranteeing: Google and Apple decide what they accept. That’s why we re-scan at 30 days and show you what changed. With the $49 audit, you get every fix step to do it yourself.

Can you guarantee AI will recommend my business?

No, and no one can. AI assistants decide on their own what to say. What we can do is show you exactly what they say today and what is within your control to fix.

How do you ask the AI?

Through each assistant's official or commercial service, with web search on. Your report lists which ones we asked and when.

Why might I see something different?

AI answers change day to day and person to person. That's why we ask more than one assistant, show every answer, and date each one.
