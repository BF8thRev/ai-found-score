Questions

What is AI Found Score?

AI Found Score is a free report for local businesses. It shows whether the AI assistants your customers use name your business when someone in your town asks for your type of service, and whether your name, phone, address, and hours match on Google, Apple, Bing, Yelp, and Facebook.

Is the report really free?

Yes. The scan and the report cost nothing. You only pay if you want the step-by-step fix guide or want us to make the fixes for you.

Do you need my passwords or logins?

No. We only read public information. We never log in to your accounts.

Which AI assistants do you check?

The ones your customers use. We ask each one the same five questions a customer in your town would ask and record every answer word for word. We add assistants over time, and your report lists exactly which ones we asked and when.

Which listing sites do you check?

Google, Apple, Bing, Yelp, and Facebook. We compare your business name, phone number, address, and hours across all five.

How long does it take?

About one business day. Leave an email after you see your questions and we send you a private link when your report is ready.

Can you fix the problems for me?

Yes. Fix it and re-check ($59) fixes your listings, gets you onto the websites AI cited, and runs the same searches we ran for your first report again thirty days later.

Can you guarantee AI will recommend my business?

No, and no one can. AI assistants decide on their own what to say. What we can do is show you exactly what they say today and fix the listing errors that are within your control.

How do you ask the AI?

Through each assistant's official or commercial service, with web search on. Your report lists which ones we asked and when.

Why might I see something different?

AI answers change day to day and person to person. That's why we ask more than one assistant, show every answer, and date each one.

Will this get me recommended?

No one can promise that, us included. We fix what's in your control, then show you exactly what changed.

Why no email or call to see my report?

Because you should be able to see what AI says about your business without being sold to first.
