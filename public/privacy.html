Privacy

Updated September 24, 2026

Business data

Reports use public information about your business: what AI assistants say and what your listings show on Google, Apple, Bing, Yelp, and Facebook. Report pages are not indexed by search engines and are reachable only by their link.

The free-report form

When you ask for a report, we keep what you type: business name, trade, town, ZIP, and your website, phone and email if you give them. We use it only to run your report and send you its link. To run it, we send the questions (your trade, town and ZIP, never your name or contact details) to the AI assistants we check. We then send their answers, with your business's public name, address, phone and website, to an AI model that finds which businesses each answer names. Your email is never sent to either.

Email

If we emailed you, your address came from a public business listing. Every email has an unsubscribe link. Unsubscribe here anytime and we stop.

Mail

If we mailed you a postcard, the address came from your public business listing. Enter the code on the card at aifoundscore.com/stop and we stop all mail and email to your business.

Your report page

When you open your report, we record the visit (time, browser type, and the page that linked you). If you ask us to email you the report, we keep that address to send it. We don't sell or share your information.

Site analytics

This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics and Google Tag Manager to count visits and button clicks. Payment pages are run by Stripe. We never see your card number.
