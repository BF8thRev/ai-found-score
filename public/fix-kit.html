What's in the kit

  • robots.txt: lets ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Google and Bing read your website.
  • llms.txt: a plain summary of your business written for AI assistants.
  • Business schema: your name, phone, address and service area in the code format Google reads.
  • FAQ page: answers to the questions people ask AI about businesses like yours.
  • Google Business Profile text: description, categories, services and service areas to copy in.
  • Review QR code: if you add your review link, a code to print on invoices and cards.
  • README: where each file goes, in plain words.
