Refund Policy

Updated September 24, 2026

The report is free. If you pay for a plan and it doesn't work out, here's how refunds work. AI Found Score is operated by Fields Holding, doing business as GetAiFound Score.

Fix steps ($29)

You get every fix from your report, step by step, with copy-paste text for your Google profile. If we can't show you 3 things you can fix, you get your money back. Email us within 7 days of purchase and we refund you in full.

Fix it and re-check ($59)

We fix your listings and get you onto the websites AI cited in your answers. Thirty days later we run the same 25 searches and show you both results.

We can't promise what AI will say, so the re-scan results are never grounds for a refund on their own. What we do promise: you'll be listed on every site we name, or you get your money back. If a site we named doesn't list you, email us within 30 days of getting your re-scan results and we refund the plan in full.

Full Year ($69)

Full Year is Fix it and re-check, plus the same 25 searches every month for a year and an email when a competitor starts getting named over you. The listing promise above applies to it the same way.

All paid work ($59, $69 and the $199 full listing build)

  • Before we start work: full refund, for any reason.
  • After we start: we refund the part of the work we haven't done. For Full Year, that includes the monthly re-scans still to come.
  • If a fix we made is wrong or doesn't hold: tell us within 30 days and we redo it at no charge. If we can't fix it, we refund that plan.

Some listing sites take days or weeks to accept changes, and a few require you to verify ownership (for example, a code sent by text, mail or phone). We'll tell you when a site is waiting on you.

How to ask

Email hello@aifoundscore.com with your business name, the email you used at checkout, and the plan you bought. For a listing refund, name the site that doesn't list you. We reply within 2 business days.

Approved refunds go back to the card you paid with, through Stripe. Your bank usually shows them in 5 to 10 business days.

Questions first

If something looks wrong on a charge, email us before disputing it with your bank. It's faster for you, and we'll make it right.
