About

AI Found Score is a small company in Plainview, NY. We run one check for local businesses: what AI assistants say about you, and whether your public listings agree.

Where the data comes from

  • AI assistants. We ask the AI assistants your customers use five questions a customer in your town would ask, through each one's official or commercial service with web search on. We record every answer word for word. We add assistants over time; each report lists exactly which ones we asked and when.
  • Listings. We read your public profile on Google, Apple, Bing, Yelp, and Facebook.

Public information only. We never log in to your accounts.

What we don't do

We can't promise what AI will say, and we don't promise placement or bookings. AI assistants and listing sites change on their own schedule. We show you what they say today and what you can fix.
