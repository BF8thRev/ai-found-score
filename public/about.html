About

AI Found Score is a small company in Plainview, NY. We run one check for local businesses: what AI assistants say about you, and whether your public listings agree.

Where the data comes from

  • AI assistants. We ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Copilot for a business like yours in your town and record the answer.
  • Listings. We read your public profile on Google, Apple, Bing, Yelp, and Facebook.

Public information only. We never log in to your accounts.

What we don't do

We don't promise rankings, placement, or bookings. AI assistants and listing sites change on their own schedule. We show you what they say today and what you can fix.
