About

Your customers used to search. Now more and more of them ask AI who to call, and they get one short answer back. If your business isn't in it, you never hear about the call you lost.

AI Found Score shows local business owners exactly what AI assistants say when a customer in their town asks for what they do: who gets named, who gets named first, which websites the AI trusted, and what the owner can fix.

Why we built it

Owners can see their Google reviews and their spot on the map. Until now, nobody could see what AI tells their customers. We think you should be able to look before anyone tries to sell you anything, so the first check is free and asks for no email.

How we work

  • Word for word. We quote every AI answer in full, with the question and the date, so you can type it in yourself and check.
  • Public information only. We read what anyone can see. We never ask for a password or log in to your accounts.
  • Nothing guessed. If we didn't find it, we don't show it.
  • Plain English. Made to read on your phone between jobs.

Who we are

AI Found Score is a small, independent company based in Plainview, on Long Island, New York. It was founded in 2026 to give local service businesses a clear, honest look at how AI sees them. You'll deal with the people who build it, not a call center.
